About

Welcome to my home page! I am a Catholic, husband, dad, and Research Fellow at the Institute for Family Studies. I live in Charlottesville, Virginia with my wife and children.

I grew up in McLean, Virginia, then attended Middlebury College for my BA in Psychology. I discerned religious life after graduation, but recognized that I felt called to marriage. Therefore, I then attended the University of Texas at Austin for a Master of Science in Social Work; after graduating, I worked as a mental health counselor with Catholic Charities of the Diocese of Evansville.

I greatly enjoyed my counseling work, but I was also interested in working in the 'back office' side of nonprofits; therefore, I obtained an MBA from Columbia Business School. While at CBS, I developed a passion for data analysis and programming; I was then able to apply that passion by working as a data analyst for Seton Education Partners, a Catholic nonprofit that manages charter schools in the Bronx and parochial schools in Ohio.

My wife and I got married in 2023, and we welcomed our first child the following year--after which we both desired to move closer to our families. Therefore, we returned to Virginia in 2024, where I began my work with the Institute for Family Studies.
